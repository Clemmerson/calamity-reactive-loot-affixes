CalamityAffixes.dll is built with the open-source components below, and parts
of them are compiled into the DLL. Their licenses require these notices to
travel with the binary. No other third-party fonts ship with this mod; visual
effects otherwise reference the base game's files. The meshes under
Meshes\CalamityAffixes are modified copies of Skyrim Special Edition meshes
(Bethesda Softworks): the trap markers with their collision removed, and the
Shadow Boxer hit effect reworked. Textures\CalamityAffixes\ShadowFistSmoke.dds
is composed from the game's own smoke textures, and Sound\FX\CalamityAffixes
is mixed from the game's own punch, swing and shout sounds. All of them are
useful only with the base game; tools/vfx rebuilds them from an installed copy.

Permissions for the mod itself are listed on its Nexus Mods page.
